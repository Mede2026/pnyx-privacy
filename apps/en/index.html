A word about me

My name is Xuan Vien Do. I build iOS apps on my own, from Quebec.

What drew me to iOS is precision: devices carrying a GPS, a barometer, an accelerometer and a gyroscope, and a system that gives you clean access to all of it. There is something satisfying about turning raw sensors into something you can read at a glance.

Both apps come from the same conviction: an app doesn't need your data to be good. Neither Pnyx nor Speed-Do-Meter has a server. Nothing is collected, nothing is resold, there is no account to create. What you produce is yours, and stays on your device or in your own iCloud.

I write every line myself, answer every email myself, and fix what's broken. If something bothers you in either app, write to me.
