Privacy Support

Numbers you can trust

Most speedometers show a number even when the sensor doesn't know. Not this one: an unreliable value shows as “--”.

🛰️

Doppler speed

Speed comes from the GPS itself, not from distance ÷ time. It is far more accurate, and doubtful readings are discarded.

⛰️

Barometric altitude

The barometer tracks changes to within a metre, against ±10–20 m for GPS. It is zeroed on GPS at the start, then follows pressure.

✈️

It knows when you're flying

An aircraft cabin is pressurised, so the barometer always reads about 2000 m. The app spots the mismatch and offers to switch to GPS.

🎯

Real G-force

Acceleration is rotated into the frame of travel: however the phone is lying, forward stays forward.

Built for skiing

Two modes at start: Normal and Ski. Riding up then down a hill isn't a run — only Ski mode splits the day.

  • Runs and lifts detected automatically, each with its own stats.
  • Cumulative vertical up and down — the number that actually means something on snow.
  • Speed zones: time spent in each band.
  • Track coloured by speed on the map, with a 3D terrain view.

Three dials, one truth

Minimal, Sport or Alpine: the look changes, the data doesn't. km/h or mph, everywhere in the app.

◯

Minimal

One thin arc, one huge number, three markers. Nothing to distract.

▮

Sport

A segmented arc that lights up step by step, with a marker on your top speed.

◔

Alpine

The dial tints with the slope and traces your last minute.

In the car, a dashboard

Turn the iPhone sideways: Apple Maps fills the screen, the speedometer moves left, and a swipeable dial gives you G-force, music or altitude.

🗺️

Apple Maps directions

Search a destination and the app switches to navigation: route on the map, turns on screen, recalculation if you stray.

🎵

Your music

Artwork, title and playback controls without leaving the app.

⌚

Standalone Apple Watch

The watch records on its own, with no iPhone nearby. Sessions sync up later over iCloud.

Nothing leaves your device

No data is collected by the developer. There is no server. Your sessions stay on your device and, if you enable it, in your own iCloud. No ads, no analytics, no third-party kits, no account to create.
