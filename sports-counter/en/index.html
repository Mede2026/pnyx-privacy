Everything that matters, nothing more

Pick your teams once. The widget shows today's games, the live score, and the next game when your teams aren't playing.

🏒

All your leagues

NHL, NBA, WNBA, NFL, MLB, MLS, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Champions League, Europa League, Formula 1 and UFC.

🔔

Useful notifications

Goals, start and end of games, a reminder before puck drop. And a special alert when one of your players scores or gets an assist.

🪟

Unobtrusive

Always on top or pinned to the desktop, three sizes, light or dark theme. It hides itself while you play full-screen games.

🇫🇷

In French

Statuses, periods and results in French. Play descriptions are translated automatically, with no account or key.

The Match window

Every detail of a game, without opening a browser. It updates itself during the game.

  • Summary: score by period, win probability, the 3 stars, game leaders, video highlights and head-to-head stats.
  • Players: the box score, with every player's stats (goals, assists, shots, time on ice, saves…).
  • Plays: every play of the game, newest first, translated into French.
  • Formula 1 and UFC: full session classification and weekend schedule, or the whole fight card.

Your favourites first

Beyond teams, follow players, drivers and fighters. They're highlighted everywhere.

  • Players: “🚨 GOAL BY CAUFIELD!” or “🅰️ ASSIST BY SUZUKI!”, with their photo.
  • F1 drivers: overtakes during the race, taking the lead, podium and win.
  • UFC fighters: reminder before the fight, walkout, win or loss.
  • Recent form: your teams' last 5 results at a glance.

Standings, playoffs and schedule

A settings window that doubles as an info hub.

🏆

Standings

Every team league, plus the F1 championship (drivers and constructors). Your teams highlighted.

👤

League leaders

The top 10 in each category: points, goals, assists, goalies, home runs…

🗓️

Schedule

Your teams' upcoming games, day by day, starting today.

Nothing leaves your computer

The developer collects no data. There is no server. Your teams and settings stay on your computer. The app only asks ESPN for scores, Google for translations and GitHub for updates — with no identifier. No ads, no analytics, no account to create.
